Lisbon · 2 days

Lisbon in two days: old town first, riverside second

Short walks, one tram, and a long dinner each evening. Everything is within 30 minutes of the center.

Wear flat shoes: the hills and cobbles are steep. Tram 28 is packed after 10:00, so ride it early or walk.

Source: sample plan invented for this page, October 2026. Hours and prices change, check before you go.
